# Use a layered auditorium for the homepage entrance

Use a scroll-driven 2.5D auditorium built from DOM, SVG, and Tailwind rather than a WebGL scene or a single enlarged photograph. Independent seat and room layers create the impression of forward travel without a 3D engine; the real schedule overlaps the last viewport to provide a continuous handoff into normal document scrolling. Hidden schedule controls stay inert until reveal, and a skip link plus a static reduced-motion entrance keep the schedule directly accessible.
